What is real here

  • The solver. Incompressible Navier–Stokes by the lattice Boltzmann method (D2Q9, BGK collision, optional Smagorinsky model). The real tab would use the same method on the GPU.
  • Obstacles from expressions. Each row is parsed and evaluated per cell. A moving point or slider changes the boundary and its velocity while the fluid runs.
  • Measured numbers. Drag and lift come from the momentum the fluid hands the body. The shedding frequency (Strouhal number) comes from the lift signal.

What it leaves out

  • Resolution. 300 × 120 cells on one CPU thread. The GPU version would have ten to fifty times the cells and finer boundaries, so its numbers would sit closer to the reference values.
  • Desmos itself. The expression list and graph paper are drawn by this page. In the plugin, Desmos draws the shaded regions, and its MathQuill fields take the input.
  • 3D. Not shown. The research package covers what Desmos 3D would allow.

What you would be deciding

  • A sixth tab, or a mode of Flow. Six tabs fit a 460 px panel only just.
  • Wind tunnel, liquid, or both. They are different solvers with different costs.
  • Fixed tank or follow the view. Following the view would change the physics every time you pan.
  • How loudly to claim accuracy. For example, whether to show the benchmark comparison by default.
